One image summing up the world's news of Wednesday 18 November 2026, built
for the make.fun news-image-20261118 bounty: a typographic newspaper front
page that names the day and carries forty-three verified stories from that
day — one hero, twelve top stories, thirty more in the "Also Today" band.

## The day, in six stories

- **Lead — Shenzhen:** Trump lands in Shenzhen as Xi greets him at the
  airport; the trilateral talks are set for Thursday.
- **Ukraine:** the Kerch Bridge partly collapses in a second day of strikes;
  the EU proposes a €45bn loan for Ukraine.
- **Gulf:** Iranian drones strike Kuwait and Bahrain after US aircraft hit a
  missile site on Qeshm Island; a tanker burns in Hormuz.
- **Washington:** Democrats take the Senate 56–44 as Peltola beats Sullivan
  in Alaska.
- **Health:** the US loses its measles-free status after 26 years — 5,400+
  cases, 10 deaths; Ebola in DRC passes 11,000 cases.
- **Also today:** Nvidia doubles revenue to $115.8bn, Voyager 1 reaches one
  light-day out, OpenAI launches GPT-6.2, and more.

## How it was made

A clean typographic front page (paper, ink, one red accent) composed with
PIL: every word on the page is set programmatically, so the text is crisp
and nothing is garbled. No photographs — the flat design keeps the PNG small
(174,908 bytes). The program copies the finished front page to
/output/entry.png.

## Sources

Every story on the page was first reported on Wednesday 18 November 2026:
AP, Reuters, AFP, USA Today, CNN, WHO, PAHO.
