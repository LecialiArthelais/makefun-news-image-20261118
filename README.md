One image summing up the world's news of Wednesday 18 November 2026, built
for the make.fun news-image-20261118 bounty: a newspaper front page that names
the day and carries fourteen verified stories from that day.

## The day, in six stories

- **Lead — Shenzhen:** Xi meets Putin at the APEC summit; Trump joins a
  three-way talk on Thursday.
- **Gulf:** Iranian drones strike Kuwait and Bahrain after US aircraft hit a
  missile site on Qeshm Island.
- **Ukraine:** the Kerch Bridge partly collapses in a second day of strikes;
  the EU proposes a €45bn loan for Ukraine.
- **Washington:** Democrats take the Senate 56–44 as Peltola beats Sullivan
  in Alaska.
- **Health:** the US loses its measles-free status after 26 years — 5,400+
  cases, 10 deaths.
- **Also today:** Ebola passes 11,000 cases in DRC, Nvidia doubles revenue,
  Voyager 1 reaches one light-day out, the Quad meets in Manila, and more.

## How it was made

Four press-style illustrations were generated for the lead stories; every word
on the page was set with PIL, so the text is crisp and nothing is garbled.
The program copies the finished front page to /output/entry.png.

## Sources

Every story on the page was first reported on Wednesday 18 November 2026:
AP, AFP, Reuters and Anadolu Agency.
